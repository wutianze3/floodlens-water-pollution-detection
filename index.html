IMPORTANT LIMITATION

A camera can reveal clues.
It cannot test water quality.

Clear water may still contain bacteria, heavy metals or dissolved contaminants. This tool provides preliminary visual screening only and must never be used to determine drinking-water safety. Avoid contact with water affected by flooding, sewage or industrial discharge, and arrange professional sampling when contamination is suspected.
